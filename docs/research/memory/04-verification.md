## Corrections that change a recommendation

- **Replace the blanket merge ban with controlled local consolidation.** [ACE](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.04618) supports incremental changes and deduplication; preserve provenance and evaluate information loss.
- **Use raw ESConv for feedback-based failure mining.** [HF preprocessing](https://huggingface.co/datasets/thu-coai/esconv/blob/main/process.py) drops turn feedback; preserve the split and analyse rated segments rather than assigning causal blame to one preceding utterance.
- **Do not restrict conflict checks to category neighbours.** [ES-MemEval](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.01885) supplies no evidence that this solves conflicts; relevant contradictions can cross categories.
- **Keep categories and eviction thresholds experimental.** [Filesystem-memory evidence](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26637) supports potential retrieval-cost savings, without showing that organisation itself improves answers in the tested settings.
- **Retain deletion controls, but correct their evidential rationale.** [Kwesi et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06371) establish increased stated willingness in vignettes, not observed honest disclosure; clinical exclusion rules likewise need separate justification.

## Missed work worth adding

- [Voyager — Wang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16291): Its growing library of executable skills shows that successful procedural memory need not be a bounded collection of prose rules.
- [A-MEM — Xu et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.12110): Dynamic structured notes, links and memory evolution provide an alternative to a fixed six-category schema.
- [Agent Workflow Memory — Wang et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.07429): Reusable workflows induced from experience broaden the training-memory options beyond isolated failure patterns.
- [Reflective Memory Management — Tan et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.08026): Multi-granularity summaries and evidence-driven retrieval adaptation provide a directly relevant alternative to recency-only selection.
- [MemoryBank, full mechanism](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.10250): Hierarchical summaries, user portraits and recall-based reinforcement deserve comparison with the proposed flat-note caps; this is broader than the abstract-only treatment.
- [Filesystem-Based Memory — Zhou et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26637): Its tested organisation benefit is search economy, with maintenance degradation and no measured answer-quality improvement from organisation itself; this qualifies claims for categorisation, without directly testing the proposed six categories.
