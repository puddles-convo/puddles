# Persona pilot

The policy is the same for every person: there are no per-person policy rules. The personal layer is the notes (situation, people, what_helps, goals, follow_up, preferences), which the note-taker keeps and the helper is given at the start of a session.

Policy: policy-v2 (policies/policy-v2.md). Turns per session: 6. Mode: real. Judge: judge-fallback-v1-74e8678d.
